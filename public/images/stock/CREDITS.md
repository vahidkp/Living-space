# Stock image credits

Every image in this folder is a **placeholder** for the Living Space Furniture
mockup. None of them shows the business's own work. All are free Unsplash
photos or renders, downloaded from `images.unsplash.com` (no Unsplash+), used
under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). That licence
allows commercial use and doesn't require attribution, but it's given here anyway.

Each one was cropped (products to 4:5 at 1200×1500, scenes to 3:2 at
1600×1067, the hero to 16:9 at 2400×1350 plus a 1290×2100 portrait crop for phones), re-encoded as a progressive JPEG,
and stripped of EXIF. Nothing was enlarged past its source resolution.

Three bed renders the sourcing pass found were left out, because they
carried the artist's signature. Removing a creator's mark isn't acceptable,
and showing it on another brand's site isn't either.
